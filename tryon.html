Questions

Which photos work best?

Sharp, well-lit photos where your body is fully visible. Avoid heavy filters, crossed arms over the torso, and clothing photos with busy backgrounds.

Which file types can I use?

PNG, JPEG, WebP, HEIC and HEIF.

What happens to my photos?

In this demo, your photos stay in your browser and are never uploaded. If you connect a try-on service, update this answer to describe how it stores and deletes images.

How accurate is the fit?

It is a visual preview, not a size guide. Use it to judge style and colour, and check the brand's size chart before you buy.
